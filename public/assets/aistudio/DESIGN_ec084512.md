## Brand & Style

The brand personality is rooted in genuine Portuguese hospitality: warm, familial, nostalgic, and proud of regional culinary craft. Operating under the spirit of *"Um pedacinho de Portugal no Tatuapé"*, the experience translates the physical soul of a traditional taberna—terracotta serving dishes, rustic pine furnishings, cobalt azulejo mosaic counters, and Benfica memorabilia—into a curated digital environment.

The target audience spans local gastronomists in São Paulo, families seeking hearty shared dining experiences, and admirers of Portuguese heritage who value authenticity over sterile modernism. The interface should evoke the comforting aroma of olive oil and roasted codfish, creating an intimate sense of entering an authentic casa portuguesa on Rua Cantagalo.

The design movement blends **Tactile Heritage** with **Warm Editorial Craft**. It avoids cold minimalism by incorporating warm cream paper tones, delicate azulejo filigree borders, rich forest green accents, and brass/gold culinary crests, structured with crisp contemporary usability for effortless menu reading and table reservations.

## Colors

The color palette draws directly from Portuguese national tradition, taberna decor, and artisanal ceramics:

- **Primary (`#183F32` - Verde Lusitano Escuro):** Represents the timeless, dignified green found on Portuguese facades, traditional wine bottle glass, and the exterior brick framing of the restaurant. Used for high-emphasis headers, prominent brand blocks, and active states.
- **Secondary (`#B92D30` - Vermelho Taberna):** Inspired by the national flag, red wine garnishes, cured presunto, and energetic banner stamps. Used for attention anchors, highlighted dish badges (e.g., "Especialidade do Chef"), calls-to-action, and pricing emphasis.
- **Tertiary (`#E4B64B` - Ouro Mostarda / Azeite Dourado):** A matte culinary gold evoking warm olive oil, pastéis de nata crusts, and antique brass fixtures. Used for dividers, subtle stars, secondary badges, and delicate ornamental framing.
- **Neutral Base (`#F4EAD5` - Marfim Pergaminho):** A warm, tactile cream background reminiscent of seasoned linen tablecloths and artisanal paper menus, eliminating the glare of sterile digital whites.
- **Supporting Accents:**
  - Terracotta Wood (`#7A3520`): Grounding earth tones for footers, rustic cards, and metadata.
  - Azulejo Cobalt (`#1C3D73`): Reserved exclusively for authentic ceramic motif borders and delicate maritime tile highlights.

## Typography

The typographic strategy balances editorial heritage with razor-sharp digital readability:

- **Playfair Display (Headlines & Section Introductions):** Provides high-contrast, graceful serifs reminiscent of vintage Portuguese broadsides, Azulejaria tile captions, and classical European culinary traditions.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Body, Descriptions, and Labels):** Guarantees effortless legibility for ingredient lists, preparation notes, allergen advice, and operational details. Its rounded, open letterforms mirror warm, welcoming hospitality.
- **Pricing & Menu Items:** Prices are treated with `price-display` in semi-bold/bold weights to mirror traditional printed taberna bills of fare, pairing smoothly with dotted leaders connecting item names to monetary values.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a structured 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop (`> 1024px`), an 8-column grid on tablet (`768px - 1023px`), and a 4-column compact grid on mobile (`< 768px`).

- **Grid Rhythms:** Outer margins scale from `1rem` on handheld screens to `2rem` on wide displays, preserving breathing room around decorative azulejo borders and vintage frames.
- **Menu Hierarchy:** Culinary sections (Entradas, Bacalhau, Peixes, Doces Conventuais) alternate between list layouts with dotted leaders and 2-to-3 column featured photo cards.
- **Edge Containment:** Content containers have a maximum width of `1200px` to maintain the feeling of an intimate dining room table rather than an expansive corporate portal.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is conveyed through tactile, material-inspired layering rather than aggressive digital drop shadows:

- **Tonal Parchment Surfaces:** Depth is created by placing white porcelain cards (`#FFFFFF`) or deep olive badges over the warm parchment background (`#F4EAD5`).
- **Terracotta & Olive Diffused Shadows:** For interactive floating menus and image cards, shadows employ a low-opacity, warm amber-tinted blur (`rgba(60, 30, 10, 0.08)` blur 16px, offset Y 6px), mimicking the soft overhead glow of tavern pendant filament lamps.
- **Artisanal Double-Lines & Azulejo Borders:** Structural borders replace digital bevels: fine 1px dashed or dual solid lines in `#E4B64B` (Ouro) or `#183F32` (Verde Lusitano) frame cards and specials.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a soft, handcrafted aesthetic (`roundedness: 1`):

- **Subtle Radii (4px - 8px):** Cards, dish preview thumbnails, and text containers use gentle corners that evoke handcrafted ceramic tiles and cut parchment cards.
- **Stamps and Medallions:** Badges denoting house specialties or Portucale stamps leverage classic fluted or scalloped silhouette outlines.
- **Dividers:** Ornamental section dividers mimic hand-drawn nautical waves, caravela emblems, or geometric diamond azulejo motifs.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Reservar Mesa / Ver Cardápio):** Solid deep Portuguese green (`#183F32`) background, crisp white parchment typography (`#F4EAD5`), 4px border radius, subtle hover glow with warm gold border highlight (`#E4B64B`).
- **Secondary Action (Vinhos & Bebidas):** Outlined warm button with 1.5px border in `#B92D30`, crimson text, soft cream hover fill (`#EDE1C7`).

### Cards & Menu Items
- **Prato Tradicional Card:** Crisp parchment or porcelain white background bordered by a delicate 1px gold border. Displays dish title in bold Playfair Display, price in Vermelho Taberna (`#B92D30`), accompanied by Portuguese olive/seafood ingredient tags.
- **Dotted Menu Entry:** Classic taberna menu row where the dish name connects to the price via a dotted baseline leader (`border-bottom: 2px dotted #E4B64B`).

### Chips & Badges
- **Serves 1-2 Pessoas / Sugestão do Chef:** Pill or softly rounded pill badge with cream background, `#B92D30` text, and fine crimson perimeter line.

### Input Fields & Booking Selectors
- Flat ivory fields with recessed border styling (`#D9CBB0`), turning into solid Verde Lusitano focus rings (`#183F32`) on engagement.

### Decorative Borders & Tile Elements
- **Azulejo Header Banner:** Subtle top and bottom geometric tile bands rendered in monochrome navy or faded sage, anchoring sections like "A Cantagalo 856" and "Pratos & Peixes".
